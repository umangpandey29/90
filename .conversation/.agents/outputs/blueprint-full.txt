EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & STRATEGIC PURPOSE
Class 9 students have faced significant academic confusion this academic year due to updated NCERT curricula and delayed
availability of Part 2 textbooks (such as the new Mathematics textbook Ganita Manjara Part 2). This 90-day execution blueprint
outlines a rigorous, day-by-day roadmap covering 100% of the Class 9 syllabus across Mathematics, Science, and Social Science.
Following this balanced rotation ensures zero backlogs, complete concept clarity, and thorough preparation for final examinations.
THE 3-STEP BRAHMASTRA STRATEGY
1. Smart Content Filtering
Before spending hours on long 1-shot
YouTube lectures, check video
relevance using AI summarizers or
Gemini/NotebookLM. If not essential,
study directly from NCERT textbooks.
2. Enable Horse Blinders
Stop comparing your progress with peers
or top scorers. Focus strictly on
completing your personal daily targets
without worrying about others'
schedules.
3. Micro-Scribing
After completing major chapters,
condense key concepts into a 1-page
cheat sheet using AI (ChatGPT/Gemini)
for instant future revision.
DAILY PERFORMANCE TRACKING & COMPETENCY SYSTEM
• Daily Performance Rating: Mark every completed topic as Base (weak understanding, requires urgent re-revision), Mid
(moderate clarity, requires problem solving), or Peak (full mastery & exam readiness).
• Competency-Based Question Practice: Solve CBSE Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3 competency questions alongside chapter
reading rather than delaying practice to the end.
• Equal Weightage for Language: Remember that English and other language papers carry equal weight (100 marks) in your final
score card.
